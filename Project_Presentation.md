## Slide 2: Introduction

---

## Slide 3: Problem Statement
**Heading:** Challenges in Traditional Hospital Management
- **Time-Consuming:** Long physical queues for booking appointments and finding paper files.
- **High Error Rates:** Manual entry leads to illegible handwriting and prescription mistakes.
- **Data Loss:** Physical documents are easily misplaced or damaged.
- **Lack of Coordination:** Poor real-time communication between doctors, admin, and patients.

---

## Slide 4: Proposed Solution
**Heading:** The Digital Alternative
- A centralized web portal to unify operations under a single digital umbrella.
- **For Patients:** Remote appointment booking and secure access to medical reports.
- **For Doctors:** Streamlined daily itineraries and rapid access to patient medical histories.
- **For Admins:** Complete oversight of hospital resources and staff management.

---

## Slide 5: Key Features & Work Done
**Heading:** Core Application Modules
- **Patient Portal:** Registration, doctor browsing, appointment booking, and accessing health insights/blogs.
- **Doctor Portal:** Secure dashboards to manage patient queues, write diagnostic reports, and manage status.
- **Admin Command Center:** View hospital statistics (doctors, patients, revenue), add new staff, and manage user accounts.
- **Contact System:** A built-in messaging system for patient inquiries.

---

## Slide 6: Technology Stack
**Heading:** Technologies Used in Development
- **Backend Core:** Python
- **Web Framework:** Flask (handles routing, requests, and logic)
- **Database Management:** MySQL (Relational database for storing all records)
- **Frontend Structure:** HTML5
- **Frontend Styling:** Vanilla CSS3
- **Frontend Interactivity:** JavaScript (Dynamic UI and real-time validations)

---

## Slide 7: Database Architecture
**Heading:** Highly Normalized Relational Database
The system is backed by a custom-designed structured database:
- **`users` Table:** Stores complete profiles for Patients, Doctors, and Admin (managing roles).
- **`appointments` Table:** The bridge recording scheduled dates/times between patients and doctors.
- **`reports` Table:** Stores post-consultation data including diagnosis, medicines, symptoms, and follow-up dates.
- **`messages` Table:** Captures 'Contact Us' queries.
